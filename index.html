About IFT-D Company Profile

Founded in 2012 in the heart of London's financial district, IFT-D has grown from a boutique research firm to a leading independent financial consulting institution serving clients across Europe, Asia, North America, and the Middle East. Our core focus is on transparent, rigorous data research and actionable financial insights that empower clients to make informed strategic decisions.

We specialize in professional financial information consulting across key asset classes: equity markets, foreign exchange, commodities, fixed income, and alternative investments (including private equity, real estate, and hedge funds). Our research methodology combines quantitative analysis, qualitative market intelligence, and on-the-ground industry expertise to deliver holistic solutions.

Our Financial Consulting Services

At IFT-D, we offer a comprehensive suite of financial consulting services designed to address the unique needs of institutional clients, corporations, and private individuals. Our services are backed by real-time data feeds, proprietary analytical models, and hands-on industry experience.

1. Global Market Information Research

  • Real-time tracking of 80+ global equity markets, 24 currency pairs, and 30+ commodity markets
  • Macroeconomic indicator analysis (GDP, inflation, interest rates, employment data) across 50+ countries
  • Industry cycle research with sector-specific trend forecasting (technology, energy, healthcare, finance)
  • Weekly and monthly market outlook reports with actionable insights and risk assessments
  • Customized alert systems for market-moving events (policy changes, economic releases, geopolitical risks)

2. Asset Investment Consulting

  • Personalized portfolio allocation planning based on risk profile, time horizon, and return objectives
  • Quantitative risk assessment using Value-at-Risk (VaR) and stress testing methodologies
  • Cross-border asset layout advice (tax optimization, regulatory compliance, currency risk management)
  • Long-term investment strategy formulation (5-10 year horizon) with quarterly performance reviews
  • Alternative investment evaluation (private equity, venture capital, real estate, infrastructure)

3. Enterprise Financial Advisory

  • Corporate financing consulting (debt vs. equity, capital structure optimization, cost of capital analysis)
  • Industry valuation using comparable company analysis, discounted cash flow (DCF), and precedent transactions
  • Merger & acquisition (M&A) information analysis (target screening, due diligence support, deal structuring)
  • Capital operation planning (cash flow management, working capital optimization, capital expenditure strategy)
  • Financial risk management (interest rate hedging, currency risk mitigation, commodity price protection)

4. Private Client Exclusive Service

  • Dedicated senior consultant assigned to each private client (minimum asset threshold: £1M)
  • Customized weekly analysis reports tailored to personal investment goals and risk tolerance
  • 24-hour online financial information support (emergency market events, urgent portfolio adjustments)
  • Family office services (intergenerational wealth transfer, estate planning, tax efficiency)
  • Exclusive access to private market opportunities and institutional investment products

5. Financial Risk Warning Service

  • Early risk identification for market volatility (technical analysis signals, sentiment indicators)
  • Policy change monitoring (central bank decisions, regulatory reforms, tax policy adjustments)
  • Industry black swan event preparedness (supply chain disruptions, regulatory crackdowns, natural disasters)
  • Counterparty risk assessment for financial transactions and business partnerships
  • Crisis management planning with predefined response strategies for market shocks
